Mission & Methodology

About SoilVision AI

Bridging advanced precision agricultural science and grassroots farming realities for farmers, students, and agronomists across India.

Scientific Foundation

Protecting India's Living Topsoil

Land degradation currently affects approximately 147 million hectares across India through water erosion, wind erosion, salinization, and biological organic carbon depletion. SoilVision AI was engineered to empower farmers with instantaneous, transparent, and actionable interpretations of their Soil Health Cards.

By translating complex laboratory measurements into verified sustainable practices — such as green manuring with Dhaincha and Sunn hemp, biofertilizer inoculation (Rhizobium, PSB, VAM), contour bunding, and gypsum reclamation — we help farmers protect natural soil microbiology while reducing unnecessary chemical input costs.

Sustainable First

Organic carbon replenishment and minimum tillage prioritized over blind chemical dosing.

Bilingual Support

Institutional Knowledge Framework

  • ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research):

    Apex agricultural research body setting national soil test critical thresholds and nutrient management standards.

  • KVK Network (Krishi Vigyan Kendras):

    730+ district-level farm science centers providing subsidized laboratory soil tests and field demonstrations.

  • National Soil Health Card (SHC) Scheme:

    Government program issuing 12-parameter diagnostic cards to farmers every 2 years across all agro-climatic zones.

  • NBSS&LUP:

    National Bureau of Soil Survey & Land Use Planning — provider of geospatial soil mapping of Indian agro-ecological zones.
