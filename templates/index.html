How SoilVision AI Works

From soil data to smart decisions in minutes, not days.

1

Enter Soil Data

Input your soil test parameters manually or upload your soil test report (PDF / JPG / PNG). You can also select your farm location on the interactive map.

2

AI Analyzes Results

Our scientific engine evaluates all parameters against ICAR benchmarks — pH, NPK, organic carbon, micronutrients — and identifies deficiencies, risks, and strengths.

3

Receive Your Prescription

Get a detailed soil health report with personalized crop recommendations, organic amendments, and sustainable soil improvement strategies.
